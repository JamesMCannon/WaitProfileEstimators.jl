## [0.1.0] — 2026-05-13

### Added
- `WaitProfileMethod` abstract type and three concrete estimators:
  `FIRIFit`, `McRaeThomson`, `Ferguson`.
- `hprime_beta(method, lola, dt)` and `hprime_beta(lola, dt; method = :symbol)`
  for retrieving Wait & Spies `(h', β)` at a given location and UTC datetime.
- `waitprofile(z, h', β)` for evaluating the Wait & Spies profile.
- Internal Grena (2012) solar-position algorithm for zenith angle.
- One-time warning from `McRaeThomson` for solar zenith angle outside
  the method's calibrated daytime range (`|χ| > 98°`).

### Documentation
- README with method descriptions, validity notes, references, and a
  comparison example.
- Static comparison plot (`assets/method_comparison.png`) generated by
  `examples/method_comparison.jl`.
